# Porma PH — Musinsa-style fashion marketplace concept for the Philippines

A single-file concept site (`index.html`, no build step) modelled on Musinsa's layout:
sticky header with search and section tabs, hero carousel, quick categories, a live
ranking grid with category filters, a payday-sale strip with a countdown to the next
15th/30th, a Musinsa Top 10 brand grid (based on Musinsa's published 2026 monthly TOP brand
rankings) and a Tokyo street grid of Japanese street labels, new arrivals, a Snap outfit feed,
a magazine block, a mobile bottom tab bar and a footer with Philippine payment methods.

Open `index.html` directly in a browser. Likes, bag and follows persist per browser via
localStorage. Products, prices and follower counts are illustrative; brand names are real
brands and typographic wordmarks stand in for their logos. Brand data lives in the `BRANDS`
and `JP` arrays in `index.html`.
